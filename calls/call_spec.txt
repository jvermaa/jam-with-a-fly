CALL SPEC - five 2-bar drum patterns to export from Ableton as MIDI

RULES FOR EVERY FILE
- 120 BPM, 4/4, clip length exactly 2 bars (4.0 seconds).
- Use only these MIDI notes:
    Kick 36, Snare 38, Closed hat 42, Open hat 46, Tom 45, Crash 49
- Every hit exactly on a sixteenth-note step. No swing, no humanise, no groove.
- Note length: one sixteenth. Nothing may ring past the end of bar 2.
- Velocity 110 for every hit.
- One MIDI track per file.
- Export as:
    calls/call_01.mid
    calls/call_02.mid
    calls/call_03.mid
    calls/call_04.mid
    calls/call_05.mid

HOW TO READ THE GRIDS
Each row is one bar of 16 sixteenth-note steps.
x = hit, . = rest. Steps are grouped in fours, one group per beat.
Step 1 is the downbeat of the bar.

call_01 - plain backbeat
Bar 1   Kick        x... .... x... ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
        Closed hat  x.x. x.x. x.x. x.x.
Bar 2   Kick        x... .... x.x. ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
        Closed hat  x.x. x.x. x.x. x.x.

call_02 - one voice at a time
Bar 1   Kick        x... .... x... ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
Bar 2   Kick        x.x. .... .... ....
        Snare       .... x... .... ....
        Tom         .... .... x.x. ....
        Crash       .... .... .... x...

call_03 - open hats and crash
Bar 1   Crash       x... .... .... ....
        Kick        x... ..x. x... ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
        Open hat    ..x. .... ..x. ....
Bar 2   Kick        x... ..x. x... ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
        Open hat    ..x. .... ..x. ....
        Tom         .... .... .... ..x.

call_04 - groove into a tom fill
Bar 1   Kick        x... .... x... ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
        Closed hat  x... x... x... x...
Bar 2   Kick        x... .... .... ....
        Closed hat  x... .... .... ....
        Tom         .... x.x. x.x. ....
        Snare       .... .... .... x.x.

call_05 - syncopated
Bar 1   Kick        x..x ..x. ..x. ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
        Closed hat  ..x. .... x... ..x.
Bar 2   Kick        x..x .... x... ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x..x
        Open hat    .... ..x. .... ....
        Tom         .... .... ..x. ....

NOTES
- The patterns are suggestions. They may be changed as long as the rules at the
  top hold and all six voices appear at least once across the five files.
- Do not add any other notes, tracks, automation or tempo changes.

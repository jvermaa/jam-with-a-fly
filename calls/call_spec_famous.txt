CALL SPEC (FAMOUS BEATS) - three 2-bar drum patterns to export from Ableton as MIDI

These are well-known drum patterns re-programmed on our grid. They are our own
transcriptions at 120 BPM, not copies of any recording or MIDI file, so they
will be slightly faster or slower than the originals.

RULES FOR EVERY FILE (same as call_spec.txt)
- 120 BPM, 4/4, clip length exactly 2 bars (4.0 seconds).
- Use only these MIDI notes:
    Kick 36, Snare 38, Closed hat 42, Open hat 46, Tom 45, Crash 49
- Every hit exactly on a sixteenth-note step. No swing, no humanise, no groove.
- Note length: one sixteenth. Nothing may ring past the end of bar 2.
- Velocity 110 for every hit.
- One MIDI track per file.
- Export as:
    calls/call_06.mid
    calls/call_07.mid
    calls/call_08.mid

HOW TO READ THE GRIDS
Each row is one bar of 16 sixteenth-note steps.
x = hit, . = rest. Steps are grouped in fours, one group per beat.
Both bars are identical in all three patterns.

call_06 - "stomp, stomp, clap" (as in Queen, We Will Rock You)
The stomps are the kick, the clap is the snare. One stomp-stomp-clap per bar.
Bar 1   Kick        x... x... .... ....
        Snare       .... .... x... ....
Bar 2   Kick        x... x... .... ....
        Snare       .... .... x... ....

call_07 - "boom, ba-boom, crack" (as in The Ronettes, Be My Baby)
Bar 1   Kick        x... ..x. x... ....
        Snare       .... .... .... x...
Bar 2   Kick        x... ..x. x... ....
        Snare       .... .... .... x...

call_08 - the "Amen break" main pattern (The Winstons, Amen, Brother)
The original ride cymbal is played on the closed hat.
Bar 1   Kick        x.x. .... ..xx ....
        Snare       .... x..x .x.. x..x
        Closed hat  x.x. x.x. x.x. x.x.
Bar 2   Kick        x.x. .... ..xx ....
        Snare       .... x..x .x.. x..x
        Closed hat  x.x. x.x. x.x. x.x.

NOTES
- Do not add any other notes, tracks, automation or tempo changes.

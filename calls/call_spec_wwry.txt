CALL SPEC (WWRY) - the Phase S target pattern, turned into MIDI by src/make_calls.py

Our own transcription of the "stomp, stomp, clap" pattern of Queen, We Will Rock
You, at 82 BPM (2 bars = 5.854 s, one sixteenth = 0.1829 s). The stomps are the
kick; the clap is played on the snare voice, because our six-voice table has no
clap. The file is built by us from the grid below. The grid was checked against
a drum score and a third-party MIDI arrangement (see NOTICE.md); neither is
copied or redistributed here.

RULES (same as call_spec.txt, except the tempo)
- 82 BPM, 4/4, clip length exactly 2 bars.
- Only Kick 36 and Snare 38.
- Every hit exactly on a sixteenth-note step, one sixteenth long, velocity 110.
- One MIDI track.
- Written as calls/wwry.mid.

HOW TO READ THE GRID
Each row is one bar of 16 sixteenth-note steps. x = hit, . = rest.
Steps are grouped in fours, one group per beat. Both bars are identical.

wwry - "stomp, stomp, clap", twice per bar
Bar 1   Kick        x.x. .... x.x. ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
Bar 2   Kick        x.x. .... x.x. ....
        Snare       .... x... .... x...
